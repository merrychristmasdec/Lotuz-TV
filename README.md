## ✨ Highlights

- Premium, smooth IPTV experience with a spacious, remote-friendly TV UI.
- Android and Android TV support, including an STB-style guide, groups, channel rows, and player flow.
- M3U / M3U8 playlists, Xtream Codes, and Stalker Portal / MAC Portal setup.
- Smooth playback and channel switching with HLS and DASH playback support.
- **30+ language support** through the device system-language setting.
- 🇮🇳 **JioTV support** — Indian users without a playlist can log in with their own Jio number and access only the channels available through their legitimate JioTV account or subscription.

## 📥 Installation

Public release builds and installation instructions are **coming soon**. Release announcements will appear first on the [Lotuz TV Telegram channel](https://t.me/LotuzTV). Until then, install only builds published through official Lotuz TV channels—never APKs from unverified sources.

## ⚙️ Configuration

1. Open **Add playlist**.
2. Select **M3U playlist**, **Xtream Codes**, or **Stalker Portal / MAC Portal**.
3. Enter details supplied by your legitimate IPTV provider or subscription.
4. If you use JioTV, choose **Login with Jio** and authenticate with your own Jio number. Channel availability remains subject to your account entitlement.

Do not add, share, or commit playlist URLs that contain passwords, access tokens, cookies, or other credentials.

## 🔒 Closed-Source Project

Lotuz TV is not open source. The app is being developed privately, so code contributions and source builds are not available. You can still help by joining the community, sharing feedback, reporting issues after release, and supporting the official Telegram channel.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

- Provider credentials and account sessions are private—keep them out of issues, pull requests, logs, and screenshots.
- Use only accounts and subscriptions you are authorized to use.
- Review any playlist source before adding it, especially URLs that contain query parameters or headers.
